From Space to Action

Agricultural Drought Early Warning System — Oromia, Ethiopia

This dashboard is a prototype demonstrating an AI-powered drought prediction pipeline for the Oromia Region of Ethiopia, focusing on the Arsi-Bale pilot zone. The system processes multi-source Earth Observation data to predict drought severity classes up to 30 days in advance.

Methodology

The system ingests multi-source satellite data from Google Earth Engine (GEE): Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery for vegetation indices (NDVI, EVI, VCI), CHIRPS precipitation estimates, and ERA5-Land reanalysis for soil moisture and temperature.

Features are engineered with time-lagged sequences, rolling statistics, and drought indices (SPI, PDSI). Four models are trained and benchmarked: a VCI-based Rule Baseline, Random Forest, XGBoost with SHAP explainability, and a PyTorch LSTM for sequential patterns.

Models are evaluated on held-out temporal splits to prevent data leakage, using Macro-F1, Probability of Detection (POD), and False Alarm Ratio (FAR) metrics.

Research Prototype Disclaimer
This is an educational research prototype. All data displayed in MVP mode is synthetically generated for demonstration purposes only. Do not use for official agricultural planning or disaster response decisions.

  • PyTorch (LSTM Neural Network)

  • Plotly.js (Visualization)

  • ERA5-Land Reanalysis (ECMWF)
  • MODIS NDVI MOD13Q1 (NASA)
